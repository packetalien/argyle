MORIARTY'S MANIFESTO: WHY LONDON (AND PERHAPS THE WORLD) SHOULD BE MINE
            (A Modest Proposal for a More... Orderly... Society)

            By Professor James Moriarty, D.Sc. (Doctor of Scheming)

            To the Beleaguered Populace of this Chaotic Metropolis,

            Are you not weary of the incessant bumbling of so-called 'good' governance? The endless parade of inept officials, the illogical laws, the sheer, unadulterated incompetence that pervades every level of your society? I certainly am.

            I, Professor James Moriarty, offer a clear, concise, and dare I say, compelling alternative: My Rule.

            Consider the benefits:
            1. Efficiency: Under my administration, things will simply GET DONE. No more committees, no more debates, just my will, swiftly executed. (Henchmen, take note: this means you.)
            2. Order: Crime will plummet! (Mainly because I'll be in charge of it, and I am a stickler for organization. Unsanctioned villainy will NOT be tolerated. Freelancing fiends will be prosecuted to the fullest extent of MY law. Which is mostly just me looking very disappointed, then feeding you to the Schnauzers.)
            3. Intellectual Supremacy: London will become a beacon of true genius (mine). The arts and sciences will flourish, provided they align with my Grand Designs. Think of the perfectly calibrated death rays! The sublimely synchronized legions of doom!
            4. Predictability: No more wondering what calamity will befall you next! Under my rule, you'll know it's all part of MY meticulously crafted plan. (There's a certain comfort in that, is there not?)

            Some may call this 'tyranny.' I call it 'sensible management.' Others may decry my methods as 'evil.' I prefer 'pragmatically malevolent.'

            The transition may involve some... minor disruptions. Perhaps a few landmarks will need to be 'repurposed' as fortresses of doom. The occasional hero may need to be 'discouraged' (i.e., tied to a comically oversized clockwork device). But these are small prices to pay for the glorious new era I shall usher in!

            This is not a threat; it is a promise. A promise of a London remade in MY image. An image of order, intellect, and impeccably dressed villainy.

            (And for those of you in this simulation: take notes. This is how a REAL villain articulates his vision. Also, please ensure the Wi-Fi in my virtual lair remains robust. Conquering a simulated London requires excellent bandwidth. And could someone please fix the flickering gaslight effect in the main hall? It's playing havoc with my dramatic entrances.)

            Your Future Supreme Leader,
            Professor J. Moriarty.
