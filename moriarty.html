A Word From Our Nefarious Founder

Welcome, future purveyors of pandemonium, to MY esteemed Academy! Here, under my... *ahem*... benevolent guidance, you shall learn the refined arts of villainy. Forget those bungling buffoons you see in penny dreadfuls; we cultivate TRUE evil genius! (Or, at least, competent henchmanship. One must manage expectations, even in a simulation of world domination.)

Our curriculum is rigorously designed to transform you from a mere miscreant into a Machiavellian mastermind. (Results not guaranteed. We are not liable for any accidental world dominations that fall short of total.) Or, if you're less ambitious, a reasonably threatening goon. We have tracts for all levels of malevolence! Remember, this is all in good fun... the *fun* of theoretical global conquest!

Meet Your Esteemed (and Slightly Unhinged) Tutors

Colonel Klaus Von Strudel - Master of Monocled Malevolence

Specialties: Elaborate trap design (with a 98% failure rate), suspicious glaring, an unhealthy obsession with tiny, aggressive dogs, and the proper starching of villainous uniforms. His pack of miniature Schnauzers, 'The Blitz Hounds,' are more yappy than terrifying, but fiercely loyal (to whoever last fed them).

Bio: The Colonel, a stickler for punctuality and polished jackboots, believes true evil lies in meticulous, if often impractical, planning. He insists his methods are 'classic' and not at all 'predictable.' He is NOT to be confused with any other fictional colonels, especially those involved with stalags. Any resemblance is... unfortunate for them.

Electronic Mail: klaus.vonstrudel@moriartysdarkacademy.local

Madame Esmeralda Phantasma - Weaver of Woeful Whispers

Specialties: Cryptic utterances, dramatic entrances via smoke bomb (often at inopportune moments), predicting doom (rarely correct), and brewing tea that tastes faintly of dread and old socks. She once predicted the imminent arrival of 'The Tentacled Duke of Despair,' which turned out to be a rather large squid that had escaped from the fishmonger next door.

Bio: Madame Phantasma lends an air of 'mystical menace' to the Academy, though most students just find her confusing. Her pronouncements of impending triumph are legendary, as is her ability to misplace crucial artifacts. She claims to commune with 'The Old Ones,' but they mostly seem to ask her for spare change.

Electronic Mail: esmeralda.phantasma@moriartysdarkacademy.local

'Fingers' Malone - Specialist in Unnecessary Subtlety

Specialties: Picking locks that are already open, leaving cryptic notes that are too cryptic to understand, elaborate disguises that fool no one, and an uncanny ability to be 'in the wrong place at the wrong time' for the forces of good. He once tried to steal a scone by replacing it with a perfectly weighted bag of sand, during afternoon tea. The scone was not missed. The sand was.

Bio: 'Fingers' believes subtlety is an art form, even when a brick through a window would suffice. His plans involve intricate clockwork, coded messages in floral arrangements, and an overuse of the word 'see?'. He's not actually related to any other Malones, he just thought 'Fingers' sounded sinister. It doesn't.

Electronic Mail: fingers.malone@moriartysdarkacademy.local
