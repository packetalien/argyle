Consulting Detective Sherlock Holmes

The world's only consulting digital detective. Mr. Holmes possesses an unparalleled ability to deduce the origins of the most obscure data packets and unravel the tangled webs of digital deceit. His keen observation skills can spot a phishing email from a mile away, even if it's disguised as a missive from the Queen herself. Beware, cyber-criminals, the game is afoot! His primary adversary remains the nefarious Professor Moriarty, whose digital machinations provide a constant challenge to even Holmes's prodigious intellect.

Electronic Correspondence: sherlock.holmes@argyle.local

Dr. John Watson, Digital Chronicler

Loyal companion to Mr. Holmes and the official chronicler of the agency's most perplexing digital cases. Dr. Watson's medical training gives him a unique perspective on 'viral' threats, and his steady hand is invaluable in documenting the aftermath of a full-blown data breach. He reminds us all to back up our files regularly. He's also quite adept at reminding Mr. Holmes to clear his browser cache, a task of elementary importance.

Electronic Correspondence: john.watson@argyle.local

Mr. Mycroft Holmes, Government Systems Analyst

A man of singular observational power and deductive reasoning, often found at the Diogenes Club (if it had a secure Wi-Fi connection). Mr. Mycroft Holmes occasionally lends his considerable intellect to matters of national digital security, capable of untangling bureaucratic network topologies that would baffle lesser minds. His motto: 'Secure the mainframe, secure the Empire.' He finds the incessant beeping of network activity rather trying on the nerves, preferring the quiet contemplation of a well-encrypted ledger.

Electronic Correspondence: mycroft.holmes@argyle.local

Inspector G. Lestrade, Scotland Yard Cyber Division

A tenacious, if sometimes bewildered, Inspector from Scotland Yard's newly formed Cyber Division. While he may not always grasp the intricacies of Mr. Holmes' methods, Inspector Lestrade is relentless in his pursuit of digital miscreants, ensuring that no rogue IP address goes un-traced. He's particularly good at 'collecting evidence' (log files). He often exclaims, 'It's all just blinking lights to me!' but his dedication to catching cyber-scoundrels is undeniable, bless his cotton socks (and firewalls).

Electronic Correspondence: g.lestrade@argyle.local
